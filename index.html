Good to know

Questions, answered.

How do I block a plot?

A ₹10 Lakhs booking amount blocks your preferred plot. The balance is payable during land registration, with the exact schedule shared by the Zacs Realty team.

Can I get a loan for the villa?

Yes. Bank loan assistance is available for villa construction. Our team can guide you through the documentation and lender process.

Who manages the villa after it is built?

Zacs Realty and Zacs Hospitality can manage construction, maintenance and vacation rentals, giving owners a more hands-off second-home experience.

What can I build on my plot?

You can build up to 2,500 sq ft. Villas start at ₹4,000 per sq ft, with an optional private pool available as an extra.

How can I see the plots in person?

Share your preferred date through the site-visit form below or WhatsApp the team directly. They will confirm availability and directions.
